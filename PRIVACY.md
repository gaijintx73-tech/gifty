# Gifty Privacy Policy

**Short version: Gifty does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Everything you save stays in your browser.**

## Data stored

When you save an item, Gifty stores the title, link, image address, price, description, list, tags, note, priority, and status you provided or that was read from the page you chose to save, in `chrome.storage.local` on your own device. Lists you create are stored the same way. No account is created and no identifier is assigned to you.

## Data transmitted

None. Gifty makes no network requests of its own, uses no analytics, and has no server. The only network activity connected to Gifty is your browser loading images from their original websites when you view saved items in the dashboard, exactly as it would load any image you link to. Gifty does not proxy, log, or record those loads.

## Permissions

- `storage`: save items and lists locally.
- `activeTab`: read the title and address of the tab you choose to save.
- `scripting` and host access: read a page's title, image, description, and price only when you trigger a save (toolbar button, right-click menu, or keyboard shortcut).
- `contextMenus`: provide the right-click save entries.

Page contents are read only on pages you explicitly save, are never sent anywhere, and are stored only as the item fields listed above.

## Data removal

Delete individual items or lists in the dashboard, or remove the extension: removing Gifty deletes all data it stored in `chrome.storage.local`. Exported JSON/CSV files are files you created; delete them like any other file.

## Changes

If a future version adds optional syncing or accounts, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and syncing will require you to opt in. The local-only version will remain available.
